Launch an animal coin

Every day, thousands of animal coins launch and get vamped. On Hatchpad, an animal coin can only be launched once.

The launch deploys on pump.fun through PumpPortal and is signed by your wallet. The coin image and metadata are stored permanently on Arweave first — signed by your wallet with a message signature, free of charge (no storage deposit) for normal coin images. It is only marked launched once a real mint and transaction exist on-chain.

Opt into pump.fun creator rewards
Pledge a share of creator fees to an animal charity

How the pledge works — plainly: pump.fun pays creator fees to your wallet, and no launch setting can redirect them automatically. Hatchpad never holds or moves your fees. Turning this on writes the pledge into your coin's public description and records it under “Launched this session” below. Once you've claimed fees on pump.fun, you send the pledged share from there in one tap — directly from your wallet to the charity's own published Solana address, or through the charity's own crypto donation page for charities that don't publish one.
